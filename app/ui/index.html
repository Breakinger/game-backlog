How to connect your sheet (one time, about 2 minutes)
  1. In your Google Sheet, open Extensions → Apps Script.
  2. Delete what's in the editor, paste the connection script, and press Save.
  3. Press Deploy → New deployment, pick type Web app, set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Anyone, then Deploy.
  4. Google asks you to authorize. If it says the app isn't verified, choose Advanced → Go to (unsafe). It's your own script.
  5. Copy the Web app link and paste it above. The sheet stays private: the app pairs with the script and keeps a secret key on this PC.
