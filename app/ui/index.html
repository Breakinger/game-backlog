How to connect your sheet (one time, about 2 minutes)
  1. In your Google Sheet, open Extensions → Apps Script.
  2. Delete what's in the editor, paste the connection script, and press Save.
  3. In the editor, open Project Settings (gear icon), tick Show "appsscript.json" manifest file, go back to the editor, open appsscript.json, replace everything in it, and Save.
  4. Turn on the Google Apps Script API: and switch it On. With the manifest, this lets the script update itself whenever Masterlog does, so you never paste it again.
  5. Press Deploy → New deployment (or Manage deployments → Edit → New version if it's already deployed), pick type Web app, set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Anyone, then Deploy.
  6. Google asks you to authorize. If it says the app isn't verified, choose Advanced → Go to (unsafe). It's your own script.
  7. Copy the Web app link and paste it above. The sheet stays private: the app pairs with the script and keeps a secret key on this PC.
IGDB: covers, screenshots and trailers for console games
  1. Sign in at (turn on two-factor if Twitch asks).
  2. Name it anything (e.g. Masterlog), set the OAuth Redirect URL to http://localhost, Category Application Integration, Client Type Confidential, then Create.
  3. Open it, copy the Client ID, press New Secret and copy the secret. Paste both below and Save.

Setting one up

Xbox controller
  1. With a cable: plug it in. That's all.
  2. Wireless: open Windows Settings → Bluetooth & devices → Add device → Bluetooth, hold the small pair button on top of the controller until the Xbox button flashes fast, and pick it from the list. An Xbox Wireless Adapter works too.
PlayStation: DualSense (PS5) or DualShock 4 (PS4)
  1. With a USB cable it works straight away.
  2. Wireless: in Settings → Bluetooth & devices → Add device → Bluetooth, hold PS and Create (DualSense) or PS and Share (DualShock 4) until the light bar blinks, then pick Wireless Controller.
  3. If buttons act strangely while Steam is open, Steam is translating the controller. Either add Masterlog to Steam (below) so Steam Input handles it properly, or turn off PlayStation Controller Support in Steam's Controller settings.
Nintendo Switch Pro Controller or Joy-Con
  1. Pro Controller: plug in with a USB-C cable, or pair over Bluetooth by holding the small sync button on top until the lights run.
  2. Joy-Con: pair each one the same way. A single Joy-Con has too few buttons for everything; use both through Steam (below), which joins them into one controller.
  3. Masterlog follows button positions, not letters: the bottom button (B on Nintendo) selects and the right one (A) goes back, like everywhere else on PC.
Steam Deck or Steam Controller
  1. In Steam, choose Games → Add a Non-Steam Game to My Library, pick Masterlog, and start Masterlog from Steam. Steam Input then presents the controller as an Xbox controller, so every button above works.
  2. In the controller settings Steam shows for Masterlog, choose the Gamepad layout.
  3. On the Steam Deck itself, use the Linux version of Masterlog: see Masterlog on the Steam Deck below.
Masterlog on the Steam Deck
  1. Switch the Deck to Desktop Mode (Steam button → Power → Switch to Desktop).
  2. Open the Releases page () in the browser and download Masterlog-SteamDeck.AppImage.
  3. In the file manager, right-click it → Properties → Permissions → tick Is executable. Move it somewhere permanent, like your home folder.
  4. Right-click it → Add to Steam. Go back to Gaming Mode; Masterlog is under Non-Steam in your library, controls and all.
  5. To bring your library over, open Settings → Your PCs on your Windows PC and copy it across, or paste your sheet's Masterlog link on the Deck.

    Use Masterlog on another PC

    Copies everything from this PC to the other one in one go: the sheet connection, linked accounts and keys, covers you picked, your ambience track and your settings. No signing in or pasting links again.

    This is the new PC

    Copy everything from the PC you already use. Open Masterlog there too.

    Through your sheet (any network)

    On the new PC, paste the link from your sheet's Masterlog PCs tab, then tick Allow next to the code it shows. Untick it later to disconnect that PC. Your PCs then keep the All Time Classic, hidden games, DLC marks, covers, ambience track and account keys the same through the sheet's connection script (version 4). Account keys travel sealed: only your PCs can open them, not Google and not the script.

    Over Wi-Fi (both PCs at home)

    The buttons above copy everything straight from one PC to the other on the same network, encrypted, never through the internet. You confirm a six-digit code on both screens first.
