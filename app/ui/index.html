How to connect your sheet (one time, about 2 minutes)
  1. In your Google Sheet, open Extensions → Apps Script.
  2. Delete what's in the editor, paste the connection script, and press Save.
  3. In the editor, open Project Settings (gear icon), tick Show "appsscript.json" manifest file, go back to the editor, open appsscript.json, replace everything in it, and Save.
  4. Turn on the Google Apps Script API: and switch it On. With the manifest, this lets the script update itself whenever Masterlog does, so you never paste it again.
  5. Press Deploy → New deployment (or Manage deployments → Edit → New version if it's already deployed), pick type Web app, set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Anyone, then Deploy.
  6. Google asks you to authorize. If it says the app isn't verified, choose Advanced → Go to (unsafe). It's your own script.
  7. Copy the Web app link and paste it above. The sheet stays private: the app pairs with the script and keeps a secret key on this PC.
IGDB: covers, screenshots and trailers for console games
  1. Sign in at (turn on two-factor if Twitch asks).
  2. Name it anything (e.g. Masterlog), set the OAuth Redirect URL to http://localhost, Category Application Integration, Client Type Confidential, then Create.
  3. Open it, copy the Client ID, press New Secret and copy the secret. Paste both below and Save.
Nexus Mods: list the mods you track and endorse (free)
  1. Signed in to Nexus Mods, open .
  2. Scroll to the bottom, copy your Personal API Key and paste it below. Masterlog only reads with it; it never downloads or changes anything.
Reddit

Reddit stopped handing out new app IDs in November 2025, so you don't need one: Masterlog reads the subreddits through its own browser engine when Reddit blocks plain requests. If you already have an approved Reddit app, you can still paste its ID here.

Linked accounts add new games on their own, track hours, and award 100% and Platinum badges. Everything is read-only, and keys are encrypted with Windows' own protection on this PC. They never go to the sheet or anywhere else.

Optional: eBay prices for physical copies
  1. Sign up free at developer.ebay.com and open Application Keys.
  2. Create a Production keyset and copy the App ID (Client ID) and Cert ID (Client Secret).
  3. Paste both below and Save. Masterlog only searches listings; it can't buy or bid.

Prices are checked every few hours and when you open Deals. The Steam Winter Sale and Summer Sale get a little celebration when they start.

Pick an audio file on this PC (MP3, OGG, M4A, WAV). If it carries intro and loop marks, the intro plays at startup and the rest loops seamlessly. The file stays on this PC. Ambience pauses when Masterlog isn't the window you're using.

Use Masterlog on another PC

Copies everything from this PC to the other one in one go: the sheet connection, linked accounts and keys, covers you picked, your ambience track and your settings. No signing in or pasting links again.

This is the new PC

Copy everything from the PC you already use. Open Masterlog there too.

Through your sheet (any network)

On the new PC, paste the link from your sheet's Masterlog PCs tab, then tick Allow next to the code it shows. Untick it later to disconnect that PC. Your PCs then keep the All Time Classic, hidden games, DLC marks, covers, ambience track and account keys the same through the sheet's connection script (version 4). Account keys travel sealed: only your PCs can open them, not Google and not the script.

Over Wi-Fi (both PCs at home)

The buttons above copy everything straight from one PC to the other on the same network, encrypted, never through the internet. You confirm a six-digit code on both screens first.
